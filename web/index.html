How it works

  1. Start a crew and invite the friends who drive you.
  2. Every time someone gives you a lift, log it. They bank a Mehmmy and the real road distance.
  3. You set the rewards: a coffee at 3, a gift card at 5, the big one at 10. They cash in when they're ready.

Rewards are promises between friends. Mehmmy never handles money.
