Support

How do friends join my crew?

Open Me and share your invite link. Friends who tap it get the app with your code ready to go, or they can tap Join with code and enter it.

How is distance measured?

Two ways. Track uses your phone's GPS during the ride and records the real distance; your route stays on your phone and only the distance is saved. Log uses the Apple Maps driving route between two saved places. Distances show in miles or kilometres based on your region; you can change it in Me → Settings.

Can someone else help run my crew?

Yes. In Me, tap the ⋯ next to a driver and choose Make co-owner. Co-owners can log rides, ask for rides and edit places and rewards. Only you can add or remove co-owners.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what gets deleted.
