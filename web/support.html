Support

How do friends join my crew?

Open Me and share your invite code or link. Friends tap Join with code and enter it.

How is distance measured?

Rides use the driving route between two saved places from Apple Maps. Distances show in miles or kilometres based on your region; you can change it in Me → Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what gets deleted.
